# AgriSight Project: Presentation Slides

## Slide 1: Title
**AgriSight: Satellite-Based Crop Monitoring for Food Security in DRC Conflict Zones**
- Leveraging multispectral analysis and machine learning for targeted interventions
- April 2025

## Slide 2: The Challenge
**Food Security Crisis in DRC Conflict Zones**
- 7+ million people displaced, 3.8 million in North and South Kivu alone
- 25.6 million people (25% of population) facing acute food insecurity
- 80 million hectares of arable land with less than 10% exploited
- Ground access severely limited in conflict areas
- Critical information gaps hampering humanitarian response

## Slide 3: Project Vision
**AgriSight: Bridging the Information Gap**
- Real-time monitoring of agricultural conditions in inaccessible areas
- Early detection of agricultural stress and land abandonment
- Evidence-based targeting of food security interventions
- Objective assessment of conflict impacts on agriculture
- Support for agricultural recovery planning

## Slide 4: Technical Approach
**Four Integrated Components**
1. Data Acquisition & Preprocessing
2. Agricultural Stress Detection
3. Land Use Change Tracking
4. Decision Support Interface

## Slide 5: Satellite Data Sources
**Optimal Platforms for DRC Monitoring**
- Sentinel-2: 10m resolution, 5-day revisit, 13 spectral bands
- Landsat 8/9: 30m resolution, 16-day revisit, historical archive
- PlanetScope: 3-5m resolution for critical areas
- Sentinel-1 SAR: All-weather capability for cloud-covered regions

## Slide 6: Agricultural Stress Detection
**Multispectral Analysis for Crop Health**
- Enhanced Vegetation Index (EVI): Best performer in tropical environments
- Normalized Difference Water Index (NDWI): Critical for water stress monitoring
- Soil-Adjusted Vegetation Index (SAVI): Effective in areas with exposed soil
- Anomaly detection comparing current conditions to historical baselines

## Slide 7: Land Use Change Tracking
**Hybrid Methodology for Conflict Zones**
- Continuous Change Detection and Classification (CCDC)
- LandTrendr for gradual change detection
- Post-classification comparison for robust verification
- Specialized metrics for agricultural abandonment detection

## Slide 8: Machine Learning Approach
**Overcoming Data Limitations**
- Transfer learning to address limited ground truth data
- Ensemble methods for robust predictions
- Time series analysis for detecting subtle changes
- Segment Anything Model adaptations for field boundary detection

## Slide 9: Prototype Demonstration
**Agricultural Stress Detection in North Kivu**
- Vegetation index calculation and anomaly detection
- Stress classification and visualization
- Automated recommendation generation
- Integration with conflict event data

## Slide 10: Key Findings
**Research Insights**
- Conflict-agriculture correlation strongest in eastern provinces
- EVI outperforms other indices in tropical environments
- Multi-sensor approach necessary to overcome cloud cover
- Hybrid change detection provides most reliable results
- Phased implementation most feasible for operational success

## Slide 11: Implementation Roadmap
**12-Month Phased Approach**
- Phase 1 (Months 1-3): Foundation
- Phase 2 (Months 4-6): Core Functionality
- Phase 3 (Months 7-9): Advanced Analytics
- Phase 4 (Months 10-12): Operational System

## Slide 12: Challenges & Mitigation
**Addressing Key Limitations**
- Cloud cover → Integration of SAR data & advanced gap-filling
- Small field sizes → Strategic use of high-resolution imagery
- Limited ground truth → Transfer learning & ensemble methods
- Security constraints → Remote validation techniques

## Slide 13: Recommendations
**Strategic Implementation**
1. Begin with pilot in North Kivu province
2. Implement hybrid data strategy (optical + SAR)
3. Establish stakeholder working group
4. Develop local capacity building program
5. Adopt open-source approach where possible

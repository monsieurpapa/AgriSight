Visual Thesis
"A military operations room that grew up — precise, warm-dark, built for people who read data under pressure and need the interface to carry the weight of that responsibility without aesthetic panic."

AgriSight is not a productivity dashboard. It is an early-warning instrument. It should feel like the Bloomberg Terminal if it were designed by a cartographer: information-dense, authoritative, and deeply respectful of the analyst's cognitive load.

Two Deliberate Departures
01
Warm dark, not cold dark
Every other serious data tool goes cold dark — navy, slate, zinc, cool grays. It signals "enterprise" but also signals emotional distance. AgriSight's background is #0D0F0E, near-black with a green undertone drawn from healthy NDVI. The text is #E8E4DA, parchment not pure white. The whole surface reads like a surveyor's field map lit by a desk lamp at 2am. Warm, which is unusual in this category. Also exactly right: this is a product where the data represents human suffering. Cold distance is the wrong emotional register for analysts who are trying to care enough to act.
02
A serif in the dashboard
No NGO data dashboard uses a display serif. They all default to grotesques because "serifs are editorial, not functional." That assumption is wrong, and Fraunces proves it. Fraunces is a variable-axis serif that goes from delicate optical cuts at small sizes to authoritative heavy display weights. Using it for district names, report headers, and stat values gives AgriSight an aesthetic register that says "precision instrument" rather than "SaaS product." DM Sans does the functional UI work. Geist Mono handles the data layer. Fraunces carries the identity. This combination doesn't exist anywhere else in humanitarian tech, which is exactly why it should.
First 3 Seconds
The analyst opens the app and thinks: "This was built for me."

Not "this is impressive." Not "this is clean." The reaction is recognition. The density is right. The color coding maps directly to the IPC scale they have memorized. The font is authoritative without being cold. Nothing is trying to charm them. The interface treats them as the expert they are.
